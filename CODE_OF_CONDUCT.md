# Code of Conduct

Tori welcomes respectful participation from contributors and users. Everyone should be treated with empathy and dignity, regardless of background, identity, appearance, disability, nationality, religion, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful when discussing ideas and reviewing work.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help keep project spaces welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Unwanted sexualized language, imagery, or attention.
- Trolling or repeated disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private or identifying information without consent.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, code review, and other spaces where someone represents Tori. Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove or edit contributions, issue a warning, or restrict participation according to the severity and pattern of the conduct. Reports will be reviewed fairly, with care for the privacy and safety of those involved.

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Reina-Kousaka1). If no private method is listed, ask a maintainer how to contact them privately without posting personal details in public.
